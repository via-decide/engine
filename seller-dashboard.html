Privacy Policy

Decide Technologies Private Limited ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and applicable ONDC guidelines.

Data We Collect

We collect your business registration details (GSTIN, PAN), bank account information for settlement purposes, and transaction data (order values, UTR numbers). Payment details are stored encrypted using AES-256.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely for: (a) ONDC seller verification and compliance, (b) settlement processing and reconciliation, (c) GST-compliant invoice generation, (d) fraud detection and dispute resolution.

Data Retention

Financial records including invoices and settlement UTRs are retained for 7 years as required under the GST Act. KYC documents are retained for the duration of your seller account plus 5 years.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction, or deletion of your personal data by writing to privacy@decideindia.com. Note that financial records required for regulatory compliance cannot be deleted before the mandatory retention period.
